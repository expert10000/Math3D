# GRA-15 nonlinear fitting and uncertainty assessment

Two named local least-squares models are available on the original checksum-checked point table: exponential decay with zero baseline and logistic growth with positive capacity. Both use bounded Levenberg–Marquardt iterations, editable initial parameters, residuals, finite fitted curves on the observed x extent, cancellation and explicit failure. They report **numerical local convergence**, not uniqueness, global optimality or certified parameter values.

The residual standard deviation `sqrt(SSE/(n−p))` is a descriptive scale. A covariance matrix based on `s² (JᵀJ)⁻¹` would be a *local linearization*, valid for inferential standard errors only under additional assumptions: the named model is adequate, x is effectively error-free, errors are independent with constant variance (and normally distributed for small-sample intervals), the optimum is interior, the Jacobian is well conditioned near it, and the optimizer has found the intended basin. A small residual or a nonsingular Jacobian at one point does not establish those assumptions or exclude an alternative basin. Clipped bounds further invalidate ordinary symmetric Wald intervals.

**Decision:** withhold parameter standard errors and confidence/prediction bands for both models. Show the local solver status, residuals, observation count, degrees of freedom, excluded rows, checksum and provenance instead. Do not reuse the polynomial pointwise bands for a nonlinear model. Future optional conditional intervals require a user-visible error-model declaration, rank/conditioning and boundary diagnostics, model-specific Jacobian verification, simulation or coverage tests on noisy references, and a label that distinguishes conditional approximation from a certified guarantee. Physical-device and accessibility signoff remain separate.
