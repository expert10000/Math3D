## 2. Installed 80-cycle scene stress

The [final workflow](https://github.com/expert10000/Math3D/actions/runs/42202363445) installed Math3D, opened alternating Rice–Mele bands and hydrogen 2p density surfaces 80 times, and closed each preview. All 80 closes returned to the one baseline canvas; a simulated `WEBGL_lose_context` event was observed on cycle four. The [full trace](quantum-qi40-installed-stress-80-2026-11-06.json) has per-process and process-role samples. Four processes remained throughout. Total working set was 466,364 KiB at baseline, 551,964 KiB after cycle 20 and 557,400 KiB after cycle 80. GPU working set was 94,264 KiB at baseline, 124,900 KiB after cycle 20 and 130,752 KiB after cycle 80. Forced-GC renderer heap grew from 28,852,496 bytes at baseline to 49,305,788 bytes after cycle 80. This run demonstrates scene lifecycle behavior, but gives no RAM/VRAM budget or leak-free conclusion. Retained JavaScript objects and real GPU device loss remain untested.

The [first workflow](https://github.com/expert10000/Math3D/actions/runs/42201908960) did not reach its stress loop: its setup installed Math3D and then the Theory Lab installer crashed. The final stress setup installs only Math3D, while the separate installer job continues to probe both apps.
