# SAGE-G02 p-adic workspace acceptance

Open **Number Theory → p-adic workspace**. Choose `Zp` over a saved `ZZ` parent or `Qp` over a saved `QQ` parent. The panel can save the missing parent to the Project. Enter prime `p`, precision `N`, two exact integer or reduced-rational operands, and optionally an integral polynomial with a simple root seed modulo `p`. Run through the Docker Sage capability and save the result as pinned Project algebra evidence. The study can be reopened from its saved evidence list after Project export and import.

The versioned request bounds `p` to primes at most 31, precision to 2–16 digits, operands to 15 decimal digits per numerator or denominator, and Hensel polynomials to degree at most four with six-digit coefficients. `Zp` accepts integers; `Qp` accepts reduced rationals. Sage computes both operands, sum, product and an optional simple-root Hensel lift. Each displayed value records its exact rational source, Sage representation, valuation when known, absolute and relative precision, unit digits, precision-loss flag and one of `known-nonzero`, `undetermined` or `exact-zero`. An `O(p^N)` approximation never becomes an exact-zero claim merely because the source fraction cancels.

Project import checks the pinned `ZZ`/`QQ` definition and recomputes exact rational sources, p-adic valuations, unit digits and precision relations with independent `BigInt` arithmetic. It checks a saved Hensel root against the full polynomial congruence modulo `p^N`, the chosen seed and the reported precision. Forged precision labels, digits, roots, parents and unsupported routes are rejected.

Fixtures include `Z7` at precision 6: `3 + 4` is displayed as `7 + O(7^6)` with valuation 1, absolute precision 6 and relative precision 5. The polynomial `x² − 2` lifts seed 3 to residue 38181 modulo 117649. In `Q5` at precision 8, `1/5` has valuation −1 and absolute precision 7; adding `−1/5` yields the exact rational source zero but the displayed approximation `O(5^7)` is marked **undetermined**. A simple root with seed zero is likewise labelled with finite precision.
