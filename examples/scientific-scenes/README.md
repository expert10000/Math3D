# Scientific scenes

These are ready-to-open `.sscene` bundle folders for the desktop Scientific Field Explorer.
In **Graphs → Scientific Field Explorer**, choose a scene under **Dataset library → Built-in scenes**;
no folder picker is needed. To open a bundle from disk, choose **Browse scientific scene** and select
the `.sscene` folder itself, not its `manifest.json` file.

| Bundle | Field | Grid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vortex-volume.sscene` | Swirling 3D velocity | 21 × 21 × 17 |
| `quadratic-volume.sscene` | 3D scalar potential | 17 × 17 × 17 |
| `complex-wave-volume.sscene` | Paired 3D complex amplitude | 41 × 41 × 17 |

The Explorer also offers the larger generated 80³ vortex and attributed measured Burgers vortex
as built-in scenes. The folders here are portable small examples; their manifests and resource
checksums are pinned and checked alongside the deterministic fixture copies.
