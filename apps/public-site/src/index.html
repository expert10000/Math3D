2.0 in preparation

Connected research, with clear source limits.

The bounded Surface path is the proposed 2.0 research showcase. Quantum scene and CAS routes remain conditional on verified external scenes or a compatible Sage service. These are source-build views, not signed 2.0 installer captures.

01 / Surface

Trace a surface result to its source.

Start from a Catenoid Surface, derive a sampled Curve and Mesh, then compare local analytic and discrete curvature evidence in one Project. Saved results link to exact source generations and Workbook references; this is not a continuous ridge or convergence proof.

  1. Create Surface
  2. Analyze curvature
  3. Compare and save evidence

Current source: bounded guided evidence and restart checks exist. 2.0 gate: the exact installed candidate still needs qualification.

02 / Quantum

Keep a quantum scene and its plots linked.

With an independently verified producer-created QWZ scene bundle, inspect a reciprocal-space point and retain its selected source with the Project. No producer scene is bundled with the Gallery card.

  1. Verify scene source
  2. Inspect linked views
  3. Save Study evidence

Current source: bounded Study views exist. 2.0 limit: this conditional route is not a completed bundled flagship journey.

03 / Algebra

Carry a CAS result into a geometric view.

With a verified compatible Sage operation, carry an exact polynomial or bounded ideal result into a qualified real sample and supported Geometry or Curve view. The sampled view retains its source and method qualifications.

  1. Create algebra object
  2. Check runtime and compute
  3. Inspect derived view

Current source: typed operations and selected handoffs exist. 2.0 limit: general algebraic visualization and singularity resolution are not advertised.

Algebra source↓Qualified result↓Geometric view↓Project evidence
